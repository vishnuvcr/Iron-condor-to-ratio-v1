# Pragmatic Gate Policy — 2026-10-04

## Purpose

The project owner has authorized a pragmatic reduction in gate strictness so the research can reach a usable conclusion without repeatedly stopping for intermediate evidence-formality issues.

## Operating rule

Intermediate gate findings may be recorded as **accepted for research progression with deferred audit** when:
- the underlying numerical or methodological result is internally coherent;
- known limitations are explicitly recorded;
- no material contradiction or safety-critical research flaw is known; and
- the limitation is scheduled for the consolidated independent audit before final acceptance.

## What is not waived

The following remain mandatory:
- no concealed data or assumptions;
- no fabricated results;
- no intentional look-ahead bias;
- all material costs and slippage assumptions;
- correct mathematical and financial-sign conventions;
- reproducible code/data provenance;
- explicit disclosure of limitations;
- final independent tester audit;
- final research manuscript and reproducibility package.

## G6 disposition

The historical G6 aggregate is accepted as **working evidence for progression**. D1-D3 evidence gaps remain disclosed and are deferred to final audit.

## Tester role

The tester should avoid blocking progress solely for evidence-packaging deficiencies when the underlying work is usable and the limitation is safely documentable. The tester must still stop progression for a material mathematical, logical, data-integrity, look-ahead, or reproducibility failure.

## Final acceptance

No final trading-strategy conclusion is accepted until the consolidated tester audit is complete and the final manuscript is internally consistent with the underlying evidence.
