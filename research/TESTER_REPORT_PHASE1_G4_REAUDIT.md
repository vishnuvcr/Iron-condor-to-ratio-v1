## G4 blocker — E044

The current `data/manifests/phase1_session_rules.json` explicitly contains an `unresolved_dates` list including:

- 2021-06-28 — observed 09:07–16:00, not yet reconciled to an official special-session source.
- 2026-06-03 — six observations, partial-day coverage.
- 2026-07-01 — 09:00–15:30, requiring pre-session classification.

Yet `scripts/phase1_session_reconciliation.py` builds its `special` mapping only from `special_sessions` and never reads or rejects the `unresolved_dates` field. Its classification logic is:

- special date -> SPECIAL_SESSION
- otherwise >=300 eligible timestamps -> NORMAL_ELIGIBLE
- otherwise -> DATA_GAP_EXCLUDED

Consequently, a date can remain explicitly unresolved in the control manifest while the generated reconciliation report declares `unresolved_dates: []`.

This is a substantive contradiction in the G4 acceptance logic, not a documentation-only issue.

**E044 severity: blocking.**

The developer must either:
1. resolve/remove those dates from the control manifest with pinned official evidence, or
2. make the reconciliation algorithm consume the unresolved-date list and fail/retain them as unresolved until independently resolved.

The acceptance output must be generated by the same logic used for production classification.

### Additional G4 concern

Therefore the claimed zero-unreconciled result does not, by itself, demonstrate that the observed timestamps conform to the documented special-session windows.
